# Code of Conduct

## In one line

Be someone other people can work with. Attack problems, never people.

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome here — any experience level, background, country, language or
day job. We want a place where a first-time contributor can ask a "dumb" question
and get a straight answer.

## What we expect

- Be direct and kind at the same time. Review code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Accept that maintainers must sometimes say no, and say why.
- Keep discussion in public issues and pull requests so others can learn from it.
- Respect security disclosure rules in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What is not allowed

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Doxxing or publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained derailing, spam, or promotion of unrelated tokens and products.
- Encouraging anyone to attack the network, users, or validators.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@inazuma.network** with what happened, links, and dates. Reports go
only to the maintainers. We reply within 5 days.

Maintainers may warn, edit or remove content, or ban an account, depending on how
serious and how repeated the behaviour is. If you believe a decision was wrong,
reply to the same thread and ask for a second maintainer to review it.
